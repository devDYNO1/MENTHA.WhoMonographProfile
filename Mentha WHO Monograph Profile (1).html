MENTHA:

WHO MONOGRAPH PROFILE

Mentha × piperita L. (Lamiaceae) — Medicinal Plant Monograph

1. MEDICINAL PART

Inspect
Dried Leaves
&

Essential Oil

(Aetheroleum Menthae Piperitae)

The WHO Monographs recognize two official medicinal materials from Mentha × piperita: Dried Leaves (Folium Menthae Piperitae) & Essential Oil (Aetheroleum Menthae Piperitae). Yields specified.

2. HABITAT (GEOGRAPHICAL DISTRIBUTION)

Inspect

Commercially cultivated extensively in eastern/northern Europe and the USA. Also found and cultivated in Africa.

3. TRADITIONAL & PHARMACOPOEIAL USES

Inspect
‘IBS’
‘Catarrh / Coughs’
‘Folk Medicine’

Supported by Clinical Data: IBS (irritable bowel syndrome), digestive disorders, myalgia.

Pharmacopoeias / Traditional Systems: Dyspepsia, flatulence, intestinal colic, catarrh, coughs.

Folk Medicine: Various traditional uses, not clinically supported.

4. DOSAGE (POSOLOGY)

Inspect
  • • Dried Leaves (Infusion): Daily dosage 3–6 g, infused in hot water.
  • • Essential Oil: Daily dosage 0.2–0.4 ml internal (in capsules/suspension), 3–4 drops inhaled.
  • • External: 5–20% in topical preparation.

5. TOXICITY

Inspect

Dried Leaves: No toxic effects in mice at high single dose (4 g/kg).

Essential Oil: High doses in rats caused cerebellar cyst-like spaces (no clinical symptoms). Non-mutagenic.

6. ADVERSE EFFECTS

Inspect

Dried Leaves: None reported.

Essential Oil: Gastric complaints, recurrent muscle pain internally. Skin irritation/hypersensitivity topically.

7. INTERACTION WITH OTHER DRUGS

Inspect

Pharmacokinetic: None reported.

Acid-Suppressing Drugs: Precaution for co-administration; use only enteric-coated essential oil to prevent premature release in the stomach.

8. PREGNANCY AND LACTATION

Inspect
?

WHO Safety Precaution: No information available on teratogenic effects or safety during nursing. Not recommended without medical supervision.

9. TRADE NAME (Pharmacopoeial & Vernacular)

Inspect

Official: Folium Menthae Piperitae, Aetheroleum Menthae Piperitae, etc.

Vernacular: Peppermint (EN), Menthe poivrée (FR), Menta piperita (IT/ES), Nana (Arabic).
